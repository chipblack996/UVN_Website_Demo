Product capabilities

We manufacture uniforms to confirmed specifications rather than selling a single fixed configuration. A project can cover garment style, fabric, colour, logo application, size range, quantity, packing and delivery requirements.

Core product groups

  • Custom polo shirts and T-shirts
  • Corporate and industrial shirts
  • Workwear for factories, engineering teams and processing operations
  • Medical coats, clinic uniforms, pharmacy uniforms and scrubs
  • Jackets, aprons and coordinated uniform items

How an inquiry is handled

  1. Share the product type, expected quantity, use environment and target delivery date.
  2. Confirm the style, fabric, colours, logo technique and size requirements.
  3. Review the agreed sample and specification before production.
  4. Confirm production and delivery terms for the approved scope.

Claims about standards, protective performance, production capacity or export documentation are confirmed only when they are supported by the specific project records.
